[1.5.0]

Ported to NeoForge 26.1.2 (Minecraft 26.1.2).

Note for this version:
- Ice and Fire and Epic Knights compatibility is not in the 26.1.2 build yet, since those mods
  have not updated to 26.1. JEI, ElytraSlot, Sophisticated Backpacks, and First-Person Model
  support carry over.

Added:
- The wand now carries the glint you are editing. It updates live when you import a saved trim
  or change the preview item, so the wand in your hand always matches what is in the editor.
- Saved trim files now remember a custom display name and its color: /glint export writes them,
  and the wand's Import picker restores them when you load the trim back.

[Compat]
- Ice and Fire (Community Edition): player armor (copper, deathworm, dragonscale, sea serpent,
  troll, and the rest) now shows glints and glow outlines. CE routes this armor through its own
  renderer that bypassed the usual armor path, so it previously showed nothing.
- Ice and Fire (Community Edition): dragon, hippocampus, and hippogryph armor glints and glow
  outlines now stay on the armor instead of bleeding across the creature's body.
- Ice and Fire (Community Edition): glowing mount armor no longer leaks the back-side glow ring
  through transparent wings, feathers and fins, and water, clouds and ice behind those gaps stay
  visible instead of being hidden by an invisible plane.
- Ice and Fire (Community Edition): troll weapons now each get an outline that matches that
  weapon's shape, in hand and in the inventory, instead of every variant sharing one
  block-shaped glow.
- Ice and Fire (Community Edition): the tide trident's glow outline now traces the trident
  itself, both in hand and in the inventory slot, instead of filling a square.
- Epic Knights: armor decoration glints no longer disappear after toggling a shader pack off
  (they used to come back only after reloading the world).

Fixed:
- Glowing modded shields and other 3D-model items now trace the item's actual shape instead of
  covering the whole item with glow. This applies both held in hand and in the inventory slot.
- Held item glow outlines no longer shift or change shape as you turn the camera around them.
- Glow outlines on entities standing close together no longer bleed their colors onto each other:
  the outline of an entity in front now correctly hides the outline of one behind it.
