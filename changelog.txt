[1.5.0]

Ported to NeoForge 1.21.1.

Added:
- The wand now carries the glint you are editing. It updates live when you import a saved trim
  or change the preview item, so the wand in your hand always matches what is in the editor.
- Saved trim files now remember a custom display name and its color: /glint export writes them,
  and the wand's Import picker restores them when you load the trim back.

[Compat]
- Ice and Fire (Community Edition): player armor (copper, deathworm, dragonscale, sea serpent,
  troll, and the rest) now shows glints and glow outlines. CE routes this armor through its own
  renderer that bypassed the usual armor path, so it previously showed nothing.
- Ice and Fire (Community Edition): dragon, hippocampus, and hippogryph armor glints and glow
  outlines now stay on the armor instead of bleeding across the creature's body.
- Ice and Fire (Community Edition): troll weapons now each get an outline that matches that
  weapon's shape, in hand and in the inventory, instead of every variant sharing one
  block-shaped glow.
- Ice and Fire (Community Edition): the tide trident's glow outline now traces the trident
  itself, both in hand and in the inventory slot, instead of filling a square.
