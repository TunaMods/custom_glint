[1.6.0]

Ported to NeoForge 26.1.2.

Added:
- The Glint Table: a block for building and printing trims with a slot-based editor.
- The Chromatic design, a procedural animated oil-slick that blends up to 8 colors.
- Eight scroll directions and a manual offset for the glint animation.
- A Rainbow Dye item.
- Crafting to set a trim's glint brightness.

Altered:
- Glint Trim chest loot can now also drop Rainbow Dye and the layer and black tears.
- The 16 named glint colors now match Minecraft's dye colors exactly.
- The Glint Table and wand editor are now fully translatable, with Spanish included.

Fixed:
- The wand editor's glint-import search box now filters the list.
- Making a multi-layer trim glow no longer drops all but its first glint layer.
- Depositing a trim into the Glint Table no longer destroys it when the design or print is
  already in your library, or when the library is full.

[Compat]
- Compatible with Iris Shaders.
